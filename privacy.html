GlobalScore — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · Applies to the GlobalScore Android application (package com.runbytestudio.globalscore) published by Runbyte Studio.

Short version

GlobalScore does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Its only network activity is downloading public football data packages from GitHub.

What the app does

GlobalScore displays football fixtures and statistical score predictions that the developer publishes as read-only data packages. No model runs on your device and no data leaves your device.

Network access

  • The app requests the INTERNET permission for one purpose: downloading the public data packages (a manifest plus fixtures.json and predictions.json) from github.com/stunmuffin/globalscore-data (GitHub Releases).
  • Downloads are integrity-checked (SHA-256) against the published manifest.
  • These requests carry no user identifiers — there are none in the app.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc.) delivers the files; GitHub's own privacy policy applies to its infrastructure. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The app works fully offline: it keeps the last successfully verified package and also bundles an initial package inside the APK.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the downloaded data package in its private cache directory so it can open instantly and work offline. This cache contains no personal information. You can remove it at any time via Android Settings → Apps → GlobalScore → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

What we do NOT do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, or authentication.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage statistics.
  • No advertising or ad SDKs.
  • No device identifiers, location, contacts, or any other personal data.
  • No data sharing with third parties — there is no data to share.

Children

The app is a general-audience sports statistics viewer and is not directed at children under 13. Since no personal data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.

Data sources

Fixture data is derived from the OpenFootball football.json dataset (CC0-1.0). The app clearly shows the data cut-off date and the provenance of every prediction. Predictions are statistical estimates provided "as is"; no accuracy is guaranteed and the app provides no betting advice.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior changes in a future version, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change. The current version always lives at this address.
