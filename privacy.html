GlobalScore — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · Applies to the GlobalScore Android application (package com.runbytestudio.globalscore) published by Runbyte Studio.

Short version

GlobalScore collects no personal data and has no accounts or analytics. The app has no tracking of its own. Its network activity is (1) downloading public football data packages from GitHub and (2) loading ads from Google AdMob, which may process standard device identifiers such as the advertising ID.

What the app does

GlobalScore displays football fixtures and statistical score predictions that the developer publishes as read-only data packages. No model runs on your device and no personal data leaves your device.

Network access

  • The app requests the INTERNET permission for two purposes: downloading the public data packages (a manifest plus fixtures.json and predictions.json) from github.com/stunmuffin/globalscore-data (GitHub Releases), and loading ads.
  • Package downloads are integrity-checked (SHA-256) against the published manifest and carry no user identifiers — there are none in the app.
  • The app works fully offline: it keeps the last successfully verified package and also bundles an initial package inside the APK.

Advertising

  • Banner and rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google's privacy practices apply to ad serving; see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (for example the advertising ID) and coarse location signals as part of ad serving, in line with Google's policies.
  • The current release uses Google's official test ad unit IDs; real ads require an AdMob account and will be declared here when enabled.
  • Ads are optional: match lists and the prediction history are always free to browse. Only opening the detailed prediction of a match you have not opened before can require watching a rewarded ad, and matches you have already opened never require another ad.
  • There is no advertising on the prediction content itself, and ads are never presented as predictions or results.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the downloaded data package in its private cache directory, plus a small local state file (chosen language, remaining prediction credits, the list of matches you have opened, number of ads watched) so your choices survive restarts. None of this contains personal information. You can remove it at any time via Android Settings → Apps → GlobalScore → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app. This local state is never transmitted.

What we do NOT do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, or authentication.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage statistics of our own.
  • No device identifiers, location, contacts, or other personal data collected by the app itself.
  • No data sharing with third parties by the app — the only third parties involved are the content hosts (GitHub) and the ad provider (Google AdMob) described above.

Children

The app is a general-audience sports statistics viewer and is not directed at children under 13. No personal data is collected from anyone by the app itself.

Data sources

Fixture data is derived from the OpenFootball football.json dataset (CC0-1.0). The app clearly shows the data cut-off date and the provenance of every prediction. Predictions are statistical estimates provided "as is"; no accuracy is guaranteed and the app provides no betting advice.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior changes in a future version, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change. The current version always lives at this address.
